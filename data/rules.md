# General Rules

1. Each player may take one action per turn unless otherwise specified.
2. Initiative order determines who resolves effects first.
3. If a scenario special rule conflicts with a general rule, the scenario rule takes precedence.

## Combat and Challenges

- When a character engages with another noble, compare Grace and Charm.
- Use modifiers from abilities and events before finalizing the result.

## Victory Conditions

Players earn points by completing objectives, controlling nobles, and resolving scenario-specific events. The game ends when the final round is completed or when a faction reaches the target score.
